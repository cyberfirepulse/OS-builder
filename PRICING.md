| **Evaluation** | Trying OS-builder | 1 | **Free** – 14 days |

## How to buy

Send an e-mail to **[cyberfirepulse@gmail.com](mailto:cyberfirepulse@gmail.com?subject=OS-builder%20license)** with:

- the edition you want
- your name or organization (and invoice details if needed)

You will receive your license key (`OSB-…`). Schools and organizations can order on invoice.
For a free 14-day Evaluation license, just ask by e-mail.
